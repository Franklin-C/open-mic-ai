LocalFlow uses the following locally installed components. The model files are
quantized conversions of their named source models, not models trained by LocalFlow.

Optional dedicated decision backend: Liquid d1-omni-600M
Creator: Liquid AI
Source: https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/d1-omni-600M
License: Liquid AI LFM Open License v1.0 (included in its verified model bundle).
The original model and custom inference code are used without retraining.

Optional dedicated decision backend: Simple Jev with Qwen3.5-0.8B
Simple Jev creator: Featherless AI / Recursal AI
Source: https://github.com/featherless-ai/simple-jev
License: Apache License 2.0; LICENSE and bundled adapter notices are downloaded.
Underlying model: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B (Apache License 2.0).
The original model is used without retraining. Decisions are read from its logits;
the local application supplies a private loopback adapter and verification threshold.
Pinned model/code revisions and hashes: decisions/model-manifest.json.
Optional runtimes install PyTorch, Torchvision, Transformers, Accelerate, Pillow,
SoundFile, NumPy, FastAPI and Pydantic separately with their component licenses.

Optional EdgeRock 4B text and vision backend (experimental, original BF16 weights)
Creator: Rock Matrix; based on Qwen3.5-4B
Source: https://huggingface.co/rockmatrix-ai/EdgeRock
Runtime: https://github.com/rockmatrix-ai/EdgeRock
Licenses: Apache License 2.0; upstream LICENSE, LICENSE-CODE and NOTICE are
downloaded with the pinned checkpoint. The app does not train or alter weights.
The portable PyTorch adapter uses the upstream VisualModel implementation.
PyTorch, Torchvision, Transformers, Pillow, safetensors, accelerate, numpy,
jsonschema and LM Format Enforcer (MIT, https://github.com/noamgat/lm-format-enforcer)
are installed separately in the optional Python environment with their licenses.

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2
Creator: NVIDIA
Source: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
GGUF conversion: mudler/parakeet-cpp-gguf
https://huggingface.co/mudler/parakeet-cpp-gguf
Modification: converted to GGUF and quantized to Q8_0 or Q4_K.

Optional Parakeet TDT-CTC 110M, CTC 0.6B and TDT 0.6B v3
Creator: NVIDIA
Sources: https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt_ctc-110m
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-ctc-0.6b
https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode
GGUF conversion: https://huggingface.co/mudler/parakeet-cpp-gguf
Modification: converted to F16 GGUF (110M) or Q8_0 GGUF (0.6B).
Pinned revisions and checksums are recorded in model-catalog.json.
